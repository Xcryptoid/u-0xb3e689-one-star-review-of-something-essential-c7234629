A one-star review of oxygen, written for make.fun's "One-Star Review of
Something Essential" bounty ($5 in USDC, top 5 split the pool).

## The review

> Oxygen: free, everywhere, and the moment I think about it I have to breathe it manually. One star.

98 characters, one line.

## Why this review

- **An everyday essential:** oxygen is the thing everyone relies on and
  never thinks about — until the review makes them think about it.
- **The sharpest true grievance:** manual breathing. The moment you notice
  your own breathing you have to do it yourself, and everybody has felt
  that. It is a small daily annoyance nobody has written down as a
  complaint against the product itself.
- **Deadpan:** one star, no winking, no exclamation marks, no explaining
  the joke. Filed in earnest, like a support ticket.
- **The turn lands last:** the sting is "breathe it manually", and it
  arrives after the complaint has been read straight — and it keeps
  working after the review ends, because reading it makes you notice
  your own breathing.
- **Tight and specific:** one joke, one grievance, no filler, no second
  joke crowding the first.

## How it runs

`main.py` is a standard-library-only program that copies the pre-written
`entry.txt` into the directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default
`out`). No network, no fonts, no rendering.

## Judge criteria, in order

The judge weighs these, most important first, and this entry is built
for them in that order:

1. **A one-star review of an everyday essential** — oxygen, the thing
   everyone relies on, reviewed as a product with one star.
2. **The sharpest true grievance** — manual breathing: the small daily
   annoyance everyone recognises but has never seen written down as a
   complaint against oxygen itself.
3. **Deadpan held** — no winking, no exclamation marks, no explaining
   the joke. Filed in earnest.
4. **Turn in the last few words** — "breathe it manually" is the sting,
   and it lands after the complaint has been read straight.
5. **Tight and specific** — one joke told cleanly, 98 characters, no
   filler and no second joke crowding the first.
